# Recruitment and privacy handoff

## Before inviting candidates

Worldwide rollout uses an explicit, versioned profile per recruiting country. The reviewer selects a country from the configured approved profiles; countries without a complete profile remain closed to scored invitations and cannot start an assessment. The candidate sees the assigned terms before starting. The published careers policy identifies personaldata@mantu.com as the Group privacy contact. Confirm the applicable recruiting entity, lawful basis, language and cross-border arrangements for this service with that team. [Mantu Careers privacy policy](https://careers.mantu.com/privacy-policy)

The legal entity that decides to run this assessment owns the candidate notice and use of the results. Keep recruitment closed until the owner confirms the legal identity and contact details (and DPO, where applicable), assessment purpose, lawful basis, any legitimate interests relied on, recipients, transfer destinations/safeguards and how to get a copy, rights, complaints authority, the fixed retention window and the effects of providing or declining the assessment. Put these details in the versioned notice. Explain accommodations, human review and any alternative assessment in the invitation or notice. Record the approved wording and version outside the app.

Document the decision to use this assessment, the question calibration and job relevance, equivalent opportunities for candidates, the actual human review process and the treatment of incomplete or expired papers. The app supplies a score. Recruiters must independently evaluate the evidence, review disputes, and document decisions in their recruitment system. Perform and record the controller's DPIA screening, and complete a DPIA if the processing presents the relevant high risk. Record the processing activity, security and incident procedures and identity verification for rights requests. [GDPR, in particular Articles 5, 6, 12–22, 25, 28, 30, 32–35 and Chapter V](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng/)

## Public practice mode

Public practice is a separate self-study activity. It does not require a recruiting invitation, country notice or reviewer sign-in, and its score is not sent to recruiters or used by the recruitment assessment. A person can choose to start a new practice paper.

Practice answers and progress remain in the current page's memory until submission or page exit. On submission, the page sends the selected option IDs with an encrypted challenge. The Worker decrypts the challenge, grades the selections, and returns aggregate score information. It writes no answer or score record to R2 or Durable Object storage, sets no assessment cookie, and does not record practice answers in the browser's storage. A fresh paper can be started; a valid challenge can be scored again until it expires.

The challenge is encrypted with the separate Worker secret `PRACTICE_KEY` and expires 30 minutes after issue. The in-page score is cleared after up to 30 minutes. The application does not identify the person taking practice, but Cloudflare still processes requests and the Worker uses short-lived network rate-limit metadata. This is not a claim that the service or its infrastructure receives no technical metadata. If practice is later used to assess applicants, stop and complete the applicable controller, notice, access and retention review first.

## Optional integrity review for recruitment

A versioned country profile with `integrityReview: true` enables four distinct-question counts on a scored invitation. The browser can report a native copy with question text selected, a context menu opened on question content, or a transition to a hidden assessment page while a question is active. The server separately counts a rejected answer when the current session submits a well-formed choice that was never issued for that question at the current revision. It discards that value. An issued choice that earns zero points is an ordinary answer.

The Worker deduplicates each category per assigned question. Authorized reviewers can see the four counts after closure. Categories can overlap. Zero means no observations were received for that category. A browser can omit reports. These counts can reflect ordinary research tools, accessibility software, a calculator or a client error. They cannot identify Cluely, establish outside assistance or change the grade. The candidate sees the enabled scope before starting. Practice and default profiles collect none of these signals. Existing `copyShortcutReview: true` profiles retain their original selected-text keyboard-shortcut behavior. Expanded mode takes precedence when both flags are enabled. Each attempt keeps its original notice and deletion deadline.

The candidate can request an accommodation or raise a question with the recruiting team. Explain who reviews the counts and when records expire in the approved notice. No typed characters, clipboard contents, destination URLs, keystroke cadence, event timelines or time away are stored.

## Processing and retention decisions

Contract under the approved company account. Verify the signed DPA, current subprocessors, any restricted transfers and how applicable safeguards work. Configure named reviewer access through the company's SSO/IdP, restrict it to the designated recruiter group, and verify the live identity and role assignment before use. A real Mantu reviewer sign-in and company processor authorization have not been verified by the synthetic hosted run. The EU bindings constrain R2 residency and Durable Object execution/storage. The request path, network metadata, identity provider, account logs, exports and backups require separate review. [Cloudflare DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/) · [Durable Object jurisdictions](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/reference/data-location/)

No record can remain available beyond 60 minutes from invitation creation. An unused invitation is purged at start-by expiry. Answer edits stop at the assessment deadline; an incomplete record is retained only until the earlier of deadline plus 30 minutes or issue plus 60 minutes. A submitted record is retained only until the earlier of submission plus 30 minutes or issue plus 60 minutes. No answers or scoring records are written to Durable Object SQLite, and the R2 bucket must be private, with no public endpoint, cache, object lock, replication or backup job. The reviewer UI clears a displayed record at its cutoff and offers no routine download. Limit any rights-request export via the operator API to staff who need it. Do not copy scores to a permanent system without a separate approved purpose and retention rule.

Each record cutoff immediately prevents further application access. Individual alarms delete R2 objects; the scheduled reconciliation retries overdue records. A completed R2 delete takes effect globally for reads. Cloudflare does not promise a sub-hour bound for physical-media erasure or all network, billing, identity and security logs. The timer is an application control, not a provider-wide physical-erasure guarantee. If the requirement extends to all provider infrastructure or copies, obtain a contractual commitment before enabling countries. [R2 consistency](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/reference/consistency/) · [R2 data location](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/reference/data-location/) · [Cloudflare alarms](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/api/alarms/)

For an access, rectification, restriction, objection or erasure request, verify identity through the existing recruiting relationship, retrieve the reference there, then use the protected reviewer workspace. Review the export before disclosure and supply an intelligible account of the candidate's own data. The staff export contains assessment material such as expected answers. Store corrections and restrictions in the recruiting case; withhold the result from decisions while accuracy is contested, and revoke or reissue when appropriate. For erasure, delete the app record, verify lookup fails, and handle copies in other systems. Record only the minimum necessary evidence of fulfillment. No candidate receives an automated hiring decision from the app.
