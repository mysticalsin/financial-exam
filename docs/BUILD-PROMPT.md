## Country, retention, and staff controls

Support worldwide use through an explicit, versioned privacy profile for each approved recruiting country. Let reviewers select only countries with complete, approved profiles. An unapproved or unconfigured country stays closed for scored invitations. Require controller confirmation of the notice, lawful basis, recipients, transfer safeguards, rights contact, supervisory authority, and fixed retention before opening that country.

Use distinct hiring deadlines. Stop candidate answer edits at the assessment deadline. Purge an unused invitation at its start-by expiry. Retain an incomplete attempt's staff record until the earlier of 30 minutes after the assessment deadline or 60 minutes after invitation creation. Retain a submitted record until the earlier of 30 minutes after submission or 60 minutes after invitation creation. Enforce each cutoff on reads and with per-record deletion alarms plus a scheduled reconciliation sweep. Explain that these are application controls; provider physical-media erasure and separate network, account, identity, security and billing metadata may follow other limits. Document deletion and recovery behavior without promising a provider-wide sub-hour purge.

Provide authorized staff a protected workflow to issue invitations, set accommodations, review answers and reasoning, retrieve individual records for approved rights requests, and erase records. A person reviews results in recruitment; the app makes no hiring decision. Do not create routine downloads or permanent score copies.
